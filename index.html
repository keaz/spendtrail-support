Support

SpendTrail helps you track expenses across iPhone and Apple Watch. This page is the public support website for the App Store listing.

How to get help

If you run into a problem, open a support request on GitHub and include:

  • Your device model and iOS or watchOS version
  • The version of SpendTrail you are using
  • What you expected to happen
  • What actually happened
  • Screenshots if they help explain the issue

Frequently asked questions

How does sync work?

SpendTrail stores your data locally and uses your private iCloud account to sync data across your own devices when iCloud is available.

Can I use the app without location access?

Yes. Location capture is optional. You can deny permission or turn off location capture in Settings and continue using the app normally.

How do I fix duplicate legacy records?

Open the app, go to Settings, and use Repair Legacy Duplicates. Normal sync checks do not automatically remove ambiguous same-minute records.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete expenses directly inside the app. If you remove the app, local data is removed from the device. If iCloud sync is enabled, synced records remain in your private iCloud storage until you delete them from the app and sync again.
