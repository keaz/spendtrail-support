AI Capabilities

What the app actually uses AI for

  • SpendTrail builds a deterministic weekly insight payload from your recorded expenses before any AI generation happens.
  • That payload includes weekly totals, average-per-day, largest day, top categories, anomaly signals, week-over-week change, and forecast confidence.
  • On eligible iPhones, users can enable weekly AI insights in Settings.
  • When enabled, the app uses Apple Foundation Models through the system language model to generate a short narrative summary, up to three observations, and up to three suggested actions.
  • The prompts explicitly tell the model not to invent exact numbers, dates, currencies, or totals and to rely only on the serialized payload.

Availability

How the AI feature is gated

  • The AI narrative path is gated behind FoundationModels support and iOS 26 availability in the current build.
  • The app checks whether the current locale is supported and whether Apple Intelligence is enabled on the device.
  • If the device is not eligible, the model is not ready, the locale is unsupported, or the user disables the feature, SpendTrail falls back to deterministic insights without generating a narrative.
  • Generated narratives are saved as an insight digest and can be surfaced later, including in the Apple Watch companion.

Implementation

Architecture overview

  • The iPhone app is built in SwiftUI with a tab-based shell covering Dashboard, History, Add, Trends, Locations, and Settings.
  • Data is modeled with SwiftData and kept available locally by default.
  • When available, CloudKit sync uses the private container iCloud.com.keaz.SpendTrail.
  • Charts powers trend and breakdown views, while MapKit supports place search, maps, and location-based grouping.
  • The watchOS companion shares the same domain model and exposes quick-log, home dashboard, and sync status flows.
  • The AI narrative feature uses the on-device system language model rather than a custom remote AI backend in this codebase.

Support Relevance

Why the implementation matters for support

  • Sync issues are usually tied to iCloud availability, private database state, or delayed device reconciliation.
  • Location-related questions often come down to permission state or whether location capture is enabled in Settings.
  • Expense history and trends depend on recorded data ranges, selected filters, and category configuration.
  • Legacy duplicate repair is exposed in Settings for older ambiguous sync records.
  • Privacy questions map directly to the local-first storage model and private iCloud sync behavior.

Support

How to get help efficiently

If you open a support request, include enough detail to make the issue reproducible.

  • Your iPhone model or Apple Watch model and the iOS or watchOS version.
  • The SpendTrail app version and build if available.
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • Whether the issue appears on iPhone, Apple Watch, or both.
  • Whether iCloud sync and location capture were enabled when the issue occurred.
  • Screenshots when they clarify the problem.

FAQ

Common questions

These answers are based on the current app behavior and release documentation.

How does sync work?

SpendTrail stores data locally first and syncs across your Apple devices through your private iCloud CloudKit database when iCloud is available.

Can I use the app without location access?

Yes. Location capture is optional. You can deny access or disable it later in Settings and continue using the app normally.

What AI features does SpendTrail use?

SpendTrail can generate a weekly natural-language insight summary on eligible iPhones using Apple Foundation Models and Apple Intelligence. The narrative is based on a deterministic weekly analytics payload, not free-form model guesses.

Does AI generation depend on Apple Intelligence?

Yes. The current implementation checks device eligibility, Apple Intelligence state, locale support, and model readiness before generating the weekly narrative. If those checks fail, the app keeps deterministic insights without the AI summary.

Does the Apple Watch app work as a companion?

Yes. The Apple Watch experience is designed around quick logging and a compact daily overview, using the same synced data model when iCloud is available. It can also surface a stored insight digest generated on iPhone.

Does SpendTrail send my expense data to an external AI service?

The AI code in this project uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models system API on supported iPhones. This codebase does not show a separate custom cloud AI integration for weekly insight generation.

How do I fix duplicate legacy records?

Open SpendTrail, go to Settings, and use Repair Legacy Duplicates. That flow is intended for older ambiguous same-minute sync records.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete expenses directly inside the app. Removing the app deletes local device data. If iCloud sync is enabled, synced records remain in your private iCloud storage until they are deleted from the app and synced again.
