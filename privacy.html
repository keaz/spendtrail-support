SpendTrail

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 10, 2026

Back to Support

SpendTrail stores expense records that you create, including category assignments, timestamps, optional notes, and optional location information captured at the time of entry.

Data we collect

  • Expense amounts and currency codes
  • Category selections
  • Timestamps for created, updated, and occurred-at dates
  • Optional notes that you enter
  • Optional location information such as coordinates, place name, locality, administrative area, and country
  • iCloud sync status metadata needed to keep your devices in sync

How data is used

  • To store and display your expenses inside the app
  • To group expenses by category, date range, and location
  • To sync your data across your own Apple devices through iCloud and CloudKit
  • To troubleshoot sync issues locally on device

Data sharing

SpendTrail does not sell your data. Expense records are stored locally on your device and, when iCloud sync is enabled and available, in your private CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID.

Location data

Location capture is best effort and optional. You can deny permission or disable location capture in the app settings and still use SpendTrail normally.

Data retention

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. If iCloud sync is enabled, synced records remain in your private iCloud storage until deleted from the app and synced again.

Contact

For support requests or privacy questions, open an issue at github.com/keaz/spendtrail-support/issues.
