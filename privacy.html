SpendTrail • Privacy Policy

Privacy policy for the SpendTrail iPhone and Apple Watch app.

Effective date: March 9, 2026. This policy reflects the current release documentation for SpendTrail and describes how expense records, optional notes, optional location data, and iCloud sync metadata are handled.

Back to Support Privacy Questions

Storage

Local-first by default

SpendTrail stores the expense records you create so they remain available on your device.

Sync

Private iCloud database

When iCloud sync is enabled and available, records are stored in your private CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID.

Control

Optional location capture

Location capture is best effort and optional. You can deny access or turn it off in Settings and still use the app normally.

Data We Collect

Information stored by the app

  • Expense amounts and currency codes.
  • Category selections.
  • Timestamps for created, updated, and occurred-at dates.
  • Optional notes that you enter.
  • Optional location information such as coordinates, place name, locality, administrative area, and country.
  • iCloud sync status metadata needed to keep your devices in sync.

How Data Is Used

Purpose of the stored information

  • To store and display your expenses inside the app.
  • To group expenses by category, date range, and location.
  • To sync your data across your own Apple devices through iCloud and CloudKit.
  • To troubleshoot sync issues locally on device.

Data Sharing

How data is shared

SpendTrail does not sell your data. Expense records are stored locally on your device and, when iCloud sync is enabled and available, in your private CloudKit database associated with your Apple ID.

Retention

How long data remains

Your data remains on your device until you delete it. If iCloud sync is enabled, synced records remain in your private iCloud storage until they are deleted from the app and synced again.

AI Features

How weekly AI insights work

  • SpendTrail can optionally generate weekly narrative insights on eligible iPhones.
  • The app first computes a deterministic weekly analytics payload from your expense records, top categories, anomaly signals, and forecast data.
  • When the feature is enabled and the device supports it, the app uses Apple Foundation Models through the on-device system language model to produce a short summary, concise observations, and suggested actions.
  • The generated narrative can be saved as an insight digest for later display in the app and companion experiences.

AI Privacy

What this codebase shows

  • The current code uses Apple’s on-device Foundation Models API rather than a custom third-party remote AI service for weekly insight generation.
  • The AI feature is optional and user-controlled through Settings.
  • If the device is not eligible, the model is not ready, the locale is unsupported, or Apple Intelligence is disabled, the narrative is not generated.
  • SpendTrail continues to provide deterministic analytics even when the AI narrative feature is unavailable.

Location Data

Optional and best effort

SpendTrail can attach location context to new expenses when the feature is enabled and the device permits access. You can deny permission or disable capture later in Settings and continue using the app without location data.
